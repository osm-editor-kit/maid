What to try

  1. In the inspector, under “Relations”, click Open on the bus route. The relation opens around the inspector: the members on the left, the inspector as a card on the right that keeps the road and looks as before.
  2. Every row with › can be clicked. The selected one docks onto the card like a tab. The relation's head is a tab too: click it, and the card shows the relation's help, issues, fields and tags.
  3. Point at members: the map highlights them. Members outside the map get a Fly to button and an arrow at the map's edge. Fit and Reroute sit on the route's list (Reroute only for bicycle, hiking and foot routes, as the module supports them).
  4. Close with ✕ on the relation's head, Close on its card in the inspector, or Esc.
  5. To go up to the route master: select the relation's head, then Open on “Bus 100” under Relations in the card.
  6. Click a grey street on the map: a hint above the list says it is not in the relation and offers to add it.
  7. Scroll a list: the section headings stick to the top and stack. Click one to jump to its section.
  8. The dock only has the way table now, behind the tab at the bottom of the map.
